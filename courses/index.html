Mission One Eleven

Mission training courses

Seven open courses from the Mission One Eleven curriculum, from foundations in missiology through practicum and advanced leadership formation.

Missiology of Unreached People Groups

Weekly classes with recordings, readings and discussions. Textbooks: The Core, The Assignment with workbook. Gospel stories.

Methods of Orality

Weekly video classes. Textbooks: Building on Firm Foundations, The Core. Gospel stories and personality assessments.

Spiritual Formation in a Missional Context

Weekly classes and discussions including Counting the Cost. Textbook: Following Jesus. World religions overview.

Book of Acts

Weekly video classes with readings and chapter tests. A textbook journey through Acts.

Mentoring

Mentoring in Missions: an 11-week guided journey.

Mission One Eleven Practicum

Overseas practicum: pre-trip preparation (documents, vaccinations, first aid, policies, cultural training), post-trip debrief and final paper.

Level 2 Track

Leadership development sessions with Karl Hargestam, Tamara Gutel, Eman Hernandez, Mikael Boman and Marie Vanderwal. Discipleship, multiplication and the church circle tool.

These courses are available as open courseware. Mission One Eleven handles its certificate and enrollment pathway separately.

Messenger College

College course offering

An open Christian ethics course from Messenger College educators, presented here for study and ministry formation.

Church and Contemporary Moral Issues

Doug Petersen and Jennifer Hargestam

A Christian ethics course for contemporary church leaders: ethical reasoning frameworks, five core principles, Old and New Testament ethics, and case-study analysis. Weekly class recordings, teaching notes, readings, and guided case work.

This open course does not itself confer academic credit or a degree. Degree applications are handled directly by Messenger College.
