Who offers this course

Messenger College offers this course. On this site, Messenger College is the academic pathway partner. The home page names it the initial academic pathway partner and describes it as a Christian college.

The Pentecostal Theological Consortium (PTC) is an initiative of PCG Global Missions, Pentecostal Church of God, connected with its Global Education department and an academic pathway through Messenger College.

The teachers named for this course are Doug Petersen and Jennifer Hargestam. The teaching language is English. The level listed here is college credit.

This is the college course on the Messenger College catalog. The seven Mission One Eleven courses are a different set. They are open courseware, and you can watch and study them on this site without an account.

What this page shows

The course is described as ethical reasoning frameworks, five core principles, Old and New Testament ethics, and case-study analysis, with weekly class recordings, teaching notes, readings, and guided case work. The five principles are not named on this site.

This page is an overview. It is locked regionally pending an articulation or partnership agreement. It does not publish the lectures, the teaching notes, the readings, or the assignments. Those materials stay with Messenger College.

A rights review of any Messenger College material is still open. A locked page is an overview only. Partner names, including Messenger College, remain the property of that organization.

Credit and admission

Admission and credit are Messenger College’s decision. Apply at the college. Reading this overview does not make you a student of Messenger College, and it does not earn a PTC certificate.

Do not present this page as a grade, a transcript, or a diploma. Credit and degrees come from Messenger College, after the college admits you.

This site has no student account. It does not enroll a student, record progress, or issue a certificate. Those steps happen with the partner that owns the course.

Where to ask

Questions about a locked college course go to the college, not to a download on this site. This site does not yet publish a separate PTC email address. For the department, start with Global Education at PCG Global Missions.
