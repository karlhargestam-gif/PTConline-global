What this catalog is

This page lists Messenger College credit courses on ptconline.global. Credit courses stay with Messenger College. Each card is an overview. Full materials are not published here.

Messenger College is the academic pathway partner. The home page names it the initial academic pathway partner and describes it as a Christian college serving that pathway, helping anchor the Consortium’s vision in trusted higher education while opening doors for learning across the global mission family.

The Pentecostal Theological Consortium (PTC) is an initiative of PCG Global Missions, Pentecostal Church of God, connected with its Global Education department and an academic pathway through Messenger College.

The course listed here

One course is listed: Church and Contemporary Moral Issues. The teachers named on it are Doug Petersen and Jennifer Hargestam. The teaching language is English. The level is college credit.

The course is described as ethical reasoning frameworks, five core principles, Old and New Testament ethics, and case-study analysis, with weekly class recordings, teaching notes, readings, and guided case work. The five principles are not named on this site.

The card carries a lock badge. The course is locked regionally pending an articulation or partnership agreement.

What a locked overview means

The course page shows the overview and the lock notice. It does not publish the lectures, the notes, or the assignments. Messenger College keeps the full materials and the admission decision. A rights review of any Messenger College material is still open.

Apply at the college. Admission and credit are the college’s decision. Reading an overview does not make you a student of Messenger College, and it does not earn a PTC certificate. Do not present a page on this site as a grade, a transcript, or a diploma.

How this differs from the open courses

Mission One Eleven prepares pioneer missionaries. Its seven courses on this site are open courseware: watch, read, and study without an account on ptconline.global. Those courses run from missiology through the practicum and the Level 2 track. Messenger College courses are listed as overviews until an agreement is in place.

This site is the catalog in English, Spanish, and Portuguese. It is not the system that enrolls a student, records progress, or issues a certificate. There is no separate PTC email address on this site yet. Questions about a locked college course go to the college.
