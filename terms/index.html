Terms

Terms for using the ptconline.global catalog and open courseware.

Using the catalog

You may read the pages and study the open Mission One Eleven courses for personal learning and ministry training. That use does not make you a student of Messenger College, and it does not earn a PTC certificate.

Do not present a download from this site as a grade, a transcript or a diploma. Certificates come from Mission One Eleven. Credit and degrees come from Messenger College, after the college admits you.

Locked Messenger College pages are overviews. Do not try to reconstruct or republish materials the college has not placed here.

Materials and other services

Textbooks, videos and syllabi stay under the copyright of their owners. See the copyright page before you copy a file or post it somewhere else.

Canvas, Dropbox, YouTube, Vimeo, Mission One Eleven and Messenger College have their own terms. PTC does not control those services.

The catalog is offered as it stands. A video link or a file can change when a partner updates a course. These terms are a plain-language description and still need counsel’s review.
