Privacy

How ptconline.global handles visits, videos, links and course offers.

This site

ptconline.global is a static catalog. It has no student account and no shopping cart. Reading a page does not create a PTC profile.

The Submit a course page is the one form that keeps what you send: your name, email, country, organization, course details and files, the licence you chose, the time you agreed, and a salted hash of your internet address (not the address itself). PTC uses them to review the offer, reply, credit you if it is published, and keep a record of your permission. They are stored in a private GitHub repository and private Vercel file storage in the United States, and only the PTC review team can see them. Offers that are not published are deleted within 2 years. For published courses, PTC keeps your name for credit, and the permission record while the course is online and for 2 years after. You can ask to see, correct or delete your details, or withdraw consent, through the contact page.

The site is hosted on Vercel. The host may keep ordinary server logs, such as the address of the page, the time, and a browser identifier, in order to run and protect the service. PTC does not sell personal information.

This page describes how the site works and what PTC does with the details sent through the Submit a course form.

Videos and other sites

Class videos are played from Dropbox, YouTube or Vimeo. Those services can set their own cookies or collect usage data when a video loads or when you follow a link. Their policies govern that collection, not this page.

Links to Canvas, Mission One Eleven, Messenger College and PCG Global Missions leave this site. What you do there is covered by that organization.

Questions

To ask about this notice, use the Global Education page linked from the footer. If a direct PTC address is published later, this page will say so.
