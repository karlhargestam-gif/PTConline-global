How to enroll

How to study missionary training online, request a Mission One Eleven certificate, or apply for Pentecostal theological training at Messenger College.

1. Study an open course

Open the course and use the videos, readings and week outline. You do not create an account on ptconline.global. A local mentor can walk through the same page with you. Watching a course here does not by itself give a certificate or academic credit.

  • Browse courses

2. Certificate or cohort with Mission One Eleven

Mission One Eleven handles enrollment and certificates for its training. Use their sign-up. Canvas, linked from each open course, is where class assignments are turned in when a cohort is running.

  • Apply for certificate or enrollment

3. Credit at Messenger College

Messenger College courses on this site are locked. The page shows the overview and the lock notice, and it does not publish the lectures, notes or assignments. Apply at the college. Admission and credit are the college’s decision.

  • Apply at Messenger College
