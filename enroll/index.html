How to enroll

How to study an open course, request a Mission One Eleven certificate, or apply to Messenger College.

1. Study an open course

Open the course and use the videos, readings and week outline. You do not create an account on ptconline.global. A local mentor can walk through the same page with you. Watching a course here does not by itself give a certificate or academic credit.

On each open course, “Start the course, self-paced, free, no login” opens Week 1 on this website. This website is for self-paced, open learning. Links on the page move you from week to week. You do not need a Canvas account to watch and read.

  • Browse courses

2. Certificate or cohort with Mission One Eleven

Mission One Eleven handles enrollment and certificates for its training. Use their sign-up. Canvas, linked from each open course, is where class assignments are turned in when a cohort is running.

Canvas Lite is for a live, teacher-led class that meets on Zoom or online with a teacher. The website is for self-paced, open learning. Use Canvas when you want that live class: Zoom or online classes, discussion, assignments and a certificate.

To join a live class, create a free Canvas Lite account, choose Student, and enter the join code from Mission One Eleven. Each open course links here for those steps. Until a join code is added on that course, the cohort button opens the current Canvas course page, which asks you to log in.

Live cohorts open once at least 10 students sign up. Join the interest list with the sign-up on this page and we'll let you know when yours starts.

  • Apply for certificate or enrollment
  • Create a free Canvas Lite account

3. Credit at Messenger College

Messenger College courses on this site are locked. The page shows the overview and the lock notice, and it does not publish the lectures, notes or assignments. Apply at the college. Admission and credit are the college’s decision.

  • Apply at Messenger College
