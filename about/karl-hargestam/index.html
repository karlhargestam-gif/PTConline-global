Role

Karl Hargestam is Global Missions Director of the Pentecostal Church of God (PCG), overseeing PCG Global Missions, including the Pentecostal Theological Consortium (PTC).

The work this catalog describes

The pages of this site describe that role as oversight of PCG Global Missions, including PTC. PTC is an initiative of PCG Global Missions, Pentecostal Church of God, connected with its Global Education department and an academic pathway through Messenger College.

The catalog answers what PTC is, which courses exist, who provides them, and how long they are. Pages are published in English, Spanish, and Portuguese. The teaching videos are in English. Portuguese captions are on the videos that have a caption file.

ptconline.global is the multilingual front door and catalog. It is not the system that enrolls a student, records progress, or issues a certificate. Those steps happen with the partner that owns the course. There is no student account on this site, and reading a page does not create a PTC profile.

Education partners on the catalog

Mission One Eleven prepares pioneer missionaries. Its seven courses on this site are open courseware: watch, read, and study without an account on ptconline.global. A local mentor can walk through the same page with a student. Watching a course here does not by itself give a certificate or academic credit.

Certificates for that training come from Mission One Eleven. Canvas, linked from each open course, is where class assignments are turned in when a cohort is running. Live cohorts open once at least 10 students sign up.

Messenger College is the academic pathway partner. Its credit courses are listed as overviews until an agreement is in place. The college keeps the full materials and the admission decision. Credit and degrees come from Messenger College, after the college admits you.

Named on the Level 2 track

The Level 2 Track is open courseware from Mission One Eleven. The course description names Karl Hargestam with Tamara Gutel, Eman Hernandez, Mikael Boman, and Marie Vanderwal. The topics named with those sessions are discipleship, multiplication, and the church circle tool.

Two items on that course carry his name. The attachment “Unreached Presentation” is not posted on this site. The discussion “Remaining Faithful to What God Has Called Us To” is listed for the live, teacher-led class.

Contact

This site does not yet publish a separate PTC email address. The contact page says to start with the Global Education department at PCG Global Missions, or with the partner that runs the course you need.
