Director of PCG Global Missions

Karl Hargestam is Director of PCG (Pentecostal Church of God) Global Missions.

He leads One Chance and Operation Uttermost, a global ministry and leadership effort spanning about 110 nations.

Connected with PTC and Mission One Eleven

He is connected with the Pentecostal Theological Consortium (PTC) and with Mission One Eleven.

PTC is taking shape as a global learning network of PCG Global Missions. The aim is trusted Pentecostal teaching that can travel, while the language, mentors and ministry of each place stay intact.

Mission One Eleven prepares pioneer missionaries. Its seven courses on this site are open courseware.
