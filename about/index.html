About

What the Pentecostal Theological Consortium is, who the education partners are, and what this website does.

A shared table

Pentecostal Theological Consortium (PTC) is taking shape as a global learning network of Pentecostal Church of God Global Missions. The aim is trusted Pentecostal teaching that can travel, while the language, mentors and ministry of each place stay intact.

The pathway starts with something a leader can use: one course, in a language they understand, walked through with a local mentor and applied in real ministry.

Education partners

Mission One Eleven prepares pioneer missionaries. Its seven courses on this site are open courseware: watch, read and study without an account on ptconline.global.

Messenger College is the academic pathway partner. Its credit courses are listed as overviews. The college keeps the full materials and the admission decision.

What this website is

ptconline.global is the multilingual front door and catalog. It is not the system that enrolls a student, records progress or issues a certificate. Those steps happen with the partner that owns the course.

Pages are published in English, Spanish and Portuguese. The teaching videos are in English. Portuguese captions are on the videos that have a caption file.

Pentecostal Theological Consortium (PTC) is an initiative of PCG Global Missions, Pentecostal Church of God.
