Submit a course

Anyone, anywhere — a local church, a teacher, or a partner in any country — can offer a course or curriculum to the Pentecostal Theological Consortium.

How it works

  1. Submit. Describe the course and paste a link to the material.
  2. Review. The PTC academic team reads the offer.
  3. Edit and translate. What is accepted may be edited and translated.
  4. Publish, local to global. Accepted courses can be published free on ptconline.global, with attribution.

Your offer, in short: you keep ownership of your work and choose one of the two licences below. PTC reviews every offer and may decline it or publish only part. Published courses are free and credit you as the author. The privacy notice for this form is just above the boxes you tick.

Fields marked * are required. Add a link to the material, a small file, or both.

For example: English, Spanish, Portuguese, or another language.

Format

Paste an https link (Drive, YouTube, or another host). Put a long video there.

PDF, VTT, TXT, MD, PNG, JPG or WEBP. Up to 3 files, 3 MB each. Files are stored privately and only the PTC review team can see them.

Say how you hold the rights: you wrote it, your church holds it, or a publisher gave permission.

Bible quotations: name the translation you quote. Spanish and Portuguese PTC courses use the NVI, which is copyright Biblica: quote no more than 500 verses in total, never a complete book of the Bible, and keep Scripture under 25% of the course.

Licence

Privacy notice for this form

What we collect: your name, email, country, organization if you give one, your course details and files, the licence you chose, the time you agreed, and a scrambled (salted hash) form of your internet address to stop abuse. We do not keep your raw internet address.

Why: to review your offer, contact you about it, credit you if it is published, and keep a record of the permission you gave. We rely on your consent and on our need to keep that record.

Where: in a private GitHub repository and private Vercel file storage. Both are US companies, so your details may be stored outside your country. Only the PTC review team can see them. We do not sell your details or use them for marketing.

How long: if your course is not published, we delete your details and files within 2 years. If it is published, we keep your name for credit, and the permission record while the course is online and for 2 years after.

Your rights: you can ask to see, correct or delete your details, or withdraw your consent, through the contact page. If you are under 18, ask a parent, teacher or pastor to submit for you.
